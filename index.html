About Our Company

A Dynamic Multi-Service Company
Built on Reliability and Trust

MR QS LUXE AND SERVICES, established in 2025, is a dynamic multi-service company delivering reliable solutions across contracting, facility services, procurement, project management, and consultancy in training and education for both public and private sectors. We are a 100% black owned company and hold a B-BBEE Level 1 status — committed to transformation and excellence in every project we undertake.
